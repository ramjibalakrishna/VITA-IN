## Brand & Style

The design system embodies **Cupertino Deep Glassmorphism**: an synthesis of Apple Human Interface Guidelines—uncompromising typography, pill-shaped control ergonomics, crisp hairline borders, and strict hierarchy—merged with a celestial, dark-glass wellness interface. Designed for urban Indians navigating chronic Vitamin B12 and Vitamin D deficiencies, the UI balances clinical authority with modern luxury.

### Aesthetic Pillars
- **Midnight Luminance:** Deep twilight obsidian canvases (`#0B0F19` transitioning into `#131B2E`) pierced by soft, ambient radial luminescence representing solar daylight (Vitamin D) and bio-cellular electrical vitality (Vitamin B12).
- **Vibrant Translucency:** Frosted glass panels engineered via multi-layer blurs, high color saturation (`saturate(180%)`), and 1px hairline perimeter borders that capture specular lighting without clutter.
- **Micro-Precision Metrics:** Tabular numeric readouts, dual-ring telemetry, and solar arc trajectories that translate complex Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR) metabolic targets into glanceable bio-status cards.
- **Cultural Intentionality:** Native support for Indian dietary dynamics (lacto-vegetarianism, tannin-binding chai warnings, fermented foods) and melanin-sensitive UV-B synthesis calculations (Fitzpatrick scale I–VI) embedded directly into functional UI tokens.

## Colors

The chromatic architecture is anchored in dark mode by default, establishing a deep space where illuminated chromatic accents signal biological status.

### Role Assignments
- **Primary (`#06B6D4` / Electric Cyan):** Vitamin B12 authority token. Applied to neurological cellular metrics, B12 intake gauges, serum pg/mL charts, and primary interactive states.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B` / Solar Amber):** Vitamin D and Sunlight Synthesis token. Governs UV-B solar windows, outdoor timers, daily IU targets, and morning metabolic sync states.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` / Bio Success Green):** Optimal nutritional intake, synergistic absorption boosts (e.g., pairing D with healthy fats like ghee), and completed goals.
- **Neutral (`#94A3B8` / Slate):** Mid-tone baseline for subordinate metric units, hairline dividers, and secondary interface chrome.

### Contextual Semantics
- **Tannin & Blocker Alerts (`#FF9F0A`):** Warns against chai or coffee consumption immediately post-meals which inhibits iron, D, and B12 absorption.
- **Deficiency Critical (`#EF4444`):** Flags severe deficit thresholds below ICMR clinical baselines.
- **Fitzpatrick Phototype Spectrum (`#FDE6D2` to `#492615`):** Explicit hex levels to tune the UV-B synthesis algorithm against melanin resistance curves.

## Typography

The typography couples the sculptural geometric presence of **Poppins** for headlines and clinical data visualization with the neutral, hyper-legible mechanics of **Google Sans** for conversational instructions, nutritional logs, and fine print.

### Metric Figure Treatment
All clinical values (e.g., `1,200 IU`, `2.4 µg`, `28 ng/mL`) require tabular figure styling (`font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1`) to eliminate character jitter during real-time timeline scrolling and count-up animations.

### Case and Tracking Rules
- Micro labels and clinical markers (e.g., `ICMR RDA TARGET`, `UV-B PEAK`) must always be formatted in uppercase with wide tracking (`+0.06em`) and medium weight to retain legibility through frosted glass diffusion.
- Metric suffixes (`IU`, `µg`, `%`) sit adjacent to metric numbers at `65%` of the numeral font size with medium weight and a secondary color token (`#94A3B8`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a 12-column adaptive fluid grid engineered with a strict 8-point base spacing rhythm. Viewports must preserve 35% negative space to allow translucent backdrops to breathe.

### Form Factor Behavior
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stack with `1rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Metric rings stack vertically or sit inside a swipeable 2-up carousel. The floating quick-action dock fixes to the viewport bottom with safe area insets.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 6-column to 12-column transition with `2rem` margins. The primary telemetry (Dual Radial Rings) docks alongside the Solar Synthesis Tracker in a side-by-side bento layout.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column centered layout constrained to a maximum width of `1160px` or `1280px` for telemetry dashboards. Sections are separated by generous vertical expanses (`space-xl` to `4rem`) rather than hard horizontal dividers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on heavy drop shadows; depth is rendered through optical layering, refraction, and localized luminescence.

### Glassmorphic Material Specifications
1. **Base Layer (Canvas):** Linear gradient from `#0B0F19` (30% angle) down to `#131B2E`. Ambient decorative radial blurs (`filter: blur(80px)`) in Solar Gold (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`) and B12 Cyan (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.12)`) reside fixed in the canvas background.
2. **Elevated Surfaces (Bento Cards & Widgets):**
   - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`
   - Backdrop Filter: `blur(24px) saturate(180%)`
   - Perimeter: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`
   - Shadow: `0 8px 32px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.36), inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`
3. **Interactive Modals & Floating Docks:**
   - Background: `rgba(19, 27, 46, 0.72)`
   - Backdrop Filter: `blur(32px) saturate(190%)`
   - Perimeter: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)`
   - Shadow: `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`
4. **Active Illuminations:** Interactive states use ambient glow envelopes (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.25)` or `0 0 20px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`) instead of hard drop shadows.

## Shapes

The design system employs Cupertino squircle radii across all UI elements, establishing smooth silhouette transitions that pair naturally with frosted glass refractions.

### Geometry Hierarchy
- **Primary Bento Cards & Health Telemetry Containers:** `1.5rem` (24px) squircle radius.
- **Modals, Flyouts & Action Sheets:** `1.25rem` (20px) squircle radius.
- **Segmented Control Frames & Form Inputs:** `0.75rem` (12px) squircle radius.
- **Action Buttons, Badges, Tabs & Fitzpatrick Swatches:** `9999px` (Full Pill) or uniform circle for circular metrics.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill CTA:** High-contrast pill (`height: 48px`, `border-radius: 9999px`, horizontal padding `24px`). Background is either solid Electric Cyan (`#06B6D4`) with `#0B0F19` bold typography or Solar Gold (`#F59E0B`). Active state triggers an organic micro-scale compression (`transform: scale(0.97)`).
- **Secondary Glass Pill:** Frosted surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, text in `#F8FAFC`. On hover: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)` with subtle inward glow.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** `height: 40px`, zero border, text `#94A3B8` hover transitions to `#F8FAFC`.

### Dual Radial Progress Gauges
- Concentric or twin side-by-side circular SVG ring gauges tracking Vitamin D ($\text{IU}$) and Vitamin B12 ($\mu\text{g}$).
- Unfilled track: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` with a `10px` stroke width and rounded caps.
- Filled path: Linear gradient strokes (B12: `#06B6D4` to `#38BDF8`; D: `#F59E0B` to `#FBBF24`) accompanied by an SVG drop-shadow filter emitting an 8px diffused colored glow at the leading edge.

### Solar Arc Visualization Widget
- Parabolic inverted arc displaying the local Indian daylight curve.
- A glowing sun node animates along the trajectory based on the client device's real-time clock.
- The optimal UV-B window (e.g., 10:30 AM – 1:30 PM) is bounded by a luminous amber band along the arc with an active countdown badge.

### Segmented Diet & Habit Controls
- Enclosed frosted track (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, padding `4px`, radius `14px`).
- Segment pills: `padding: 8px 16px`, text `#94A3B8`, smooth slide transition. Active selection adopts a frosted glass surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)`), `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.20)`, and crisp `#F8FAFC` label text.

### Fitzpatrick Skin Tone Selector
- Horizontal row of 6 circular swatches (`size: 40px`, pill borders) mapped to skin phototypes I through VI.
- Active state: Surrounded by a `2px` offset neon-amber ring (`#F59E0B`) with immediate real-time recalculation of required sunlight minutes displayed underneath.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Translucent container (`height: 48px`, radius `12px`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).
- Typography: `#F8FAFC` input text with `#64748B` placeholder.
- Focus state: Border shifts to `#06B6D4` with a `0 0 0 3px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.2)` soft ambient ring.

### Cards & Nutritional Gap-Filler Tiles
- Translucent frosted glass tiles highlighting Indian food remedies (e.g., curd, fortified milk, paneer, eggs, mushrooms).
- Header contains an absorption badge: Green badge (`rgba(52, 199, 89, 0.15)`) for enhancers like healthy fats, or Amber badge (`rgba(255, 159, 10, 0.15)`) for tannin blockers like chai.
- Quantitative chip displays bioavailable yield (e.g., `+1.2 µg B12 (55% RDA)`).
